## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for zero-friction household inventory tracking under real kitchen conditions: single-handed mobile operation, glanceable shelf inspections, and split-second expiry decisions. It rejects ornamental complexity in favor of crisp utility, warmth, and immediate kinetic feedback.

### Key Dimensions
- **Personality:** Attentive, clear-headed, reliable, and optimistic. The system feels like an organized pantry rather than an enterprise spreadsheet.
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Modern Warm Functionalism. A blend of crisp Scandinavian domestic design and high-efficiency utility interfaces. It utilizes cream paper-like canvas layers, crisp elevated white product tiles, and saturated sensory cues for shelf-life urgency.
- **Target Audience:** Busy households, meal preppers, eco-conscious homemakers, and culinary hobbyists who need instant food visibility without tedious manual data entry.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Calm assurance and agency. The interface eliminates food guilt by celebrating items saved and making imminent expirations actionable rather than stressful.

## Colors

The palette establishes high chromatic differentiation to communicate food freshness states without visual noise. Surfaces emulate warm culinary workstations, while urgency tones strictly satisfy WCAG AA contrast standards (minimum 4.5:1 for body and badge text).

### Canvas & Neutral Surfaces
- **App Canvas (Warm Cream):** `#FBFBF9` acts as the overall ambient kitchen canvas, lowering glare under overhead kitchen lights.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Warm Paper):** `#F4F4F0` for segmented bars, search troughs, and inactive filter pills.
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` for primary inventory cards, modal sheets, and quick-add trays to preserve visual isolation.
- **Typography Primary (Slate Black):** `#0F172A` delivers uncompromising legibility across high-ambient lighting.
- **Typography Secondary (Muted Charcoal):** `#64748B` for secondary specs, storage timestamps, and non-critical metrics.
- **Subtle Outline (Ghost Slate):** `#E2E8F0` for crisp, non-distracting bounds.

### Shelf-Life Urgency Tiers
- **Tier 1: Fresh (4+ Days Remaining)**
  - Foreground & Stroke: `#059669` (Deep Mint/Emerald)
  - Fill / Soft Container: `#ECFDF5`
  - Visual Cue: Reassuring, stable, indicates ingredients ready for planned meal routines.
- **Tier 2: Expiring Soon (0 to 3 Days Remaining)**
  - Foreground & Stroke: `#D97706` (Vibrant Tangerine/Amber)
  - Fill / Soft Container: `#FFFBEB`
  - Visual Cue: High-salience alert nudging immediate consumption or freezer relocation.
- **Tier 3: Expired / Critical Action Needed**
  - Foreground & Stroke: `#DC2626` (Crimson Coral)
  - Fill / Soft Container: `#FEF2F2`
  - Visual Cue: Immediate prompt to discard, compost, or log consumption to maintain inventory accuracy.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the structural, geometric presence of Space Grotesk for metrics, shelf titles, and count badges with the hyper-legible, ergonomic flow of Manrope for product descriptions, dates, and micro-labels.

### Usage Standards
- **Inventory Metrics & Counters:** Render day counts, category tallies, and quantity numbers using `Space Grotesk` (`counter-display` or `headline-md`). This delivers tabular clarity at arm’s length.
- **Product Titles & Modals:** Use `headline-sm` for food names within inventory cards to maintain high density without truncated labels.
- **Micro-Badges & Expiration Tags:** Use `label-sm` or `label-md` exclusively in uppercase or strong capitalization with wide tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.02em - 0.04em`) to ensure legibility when overlaid on colored alert pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for high-density inventory views that seamlessly adapt from one-handed mobile grip to multi-column desktop pantry dashboards.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Mobile Handheld (Base):** Single-column stacked fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` vertical item gap. The bottom `4.5rem` is permanently reserved for the thumb-reachable bottom bar and Floating Action Button (FAB).
- **Tablet / Expanded Mobile (600px - 1023px):** 2-column masonry or fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Desktop Dashboard (1024px+):** Centered max-width canvas (`1280px`) using a 12-column responsive fluid grid. Left sidebar navigation (`16rem`) docks pantry filters, while the main viewport displays a 3-to-4 column inventory grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.

### Spacing Principles
- Dynamic components (cards, quick logs) use `space-md` (`1rem`) internal padding.
- Compact list items use `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) top/bottom padding to prioritize vertical scanning speed.
- Floating quick-action zones maintain at least `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) offset from bottom screen bounds to prevent interaction clashes with native device gesture navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, which look dated and muddy on kitchen counters. Instead, it relies on tonal layering, crisp micro-borders, and tactile ambient elevation.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#FBFBF9` background. No elevation or border.
- **Level 1 (Resting Inventory Cards & Containers):** Flat `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Chips):** Lifted `2px` with a defined ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action Button & Sticky Controls):** Primary emerald floating action button uses a colored depth glow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.35)`.
- **Level 4 (Sheets, Quick-Add Trays, and Overlays):** Bottom sheets slide up on a warm backdrop overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`), elevated by `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft friendliness with structured utility using `roundedness: 2` (Base `0.5rem` / `8px`).

### Shape Standards
- **Standard Cards & Modals:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) creates inviting, tactile containers that feel comfortable to tap.
- **Pill Filters, Urgency Badges & Counter Chips:** Fully rounded capsule/pill shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to immediately distinguish metadata and filters from structural inventory cards.
- **Inputs & Action Buttons:** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) provides solid structural boundaries for quick tapping and form interaction.

## Components

### 1. Inventory Food Item Card
- **Structure:** Level 1 elevation, `#FFFFFF` container with `rounded-lg` border radius and `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Content Layout:** Left side displays category icon or thumbnail; center displays item name (`headline-sm`), location badge (e.g., "Fridge"), and purchase date (`body-sm`); right side displays the Urgency Badge and 1-tap quick actions.
- **Urgency Indicator:** A 4px vertical accent stripe anchors the left border matching the item's urgency level (Emerald, Amber, or Coral Red).

### 2. Shelf-Life Urgency Badges
- **Expired:** `#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text and border, bold warning icon. Text format: `Expired` or `X Days Ago`.
- **Expiring Soon:** `#FFFBEB` background, `#D97706` text and border, clock icon. Text format: `Today`, `Tomorrow`, or `X Days Left`.
- **Fresh:** `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text and border. Text format: `X Days Left`.
- **Styling:** Height 24px, padding `0 10px`, font `label-sm`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`).

### 3. Quick-Action Resolution Trays (Swipe or Tap)
- **Consumed Button:** Soft emerald background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, fork/spoon icon. On tap, triggers a subtle celebration micro-interaction ("Eaten!").
- **Discard Button:** Soft slate background `#F1F5F9`, text `#64748B`, trash icon. Prompts a frictionless single-tap reason selector (Expired, Spoiled, Other).

### 4. Location Filter Pills (All, Fridge, Freezer, Pantry)
- **Default State:** `#F4F4F0` background, `#1E293B` text, `rounded-full`, padding `8px 16px`.
- **Active State:** `#0F172A` deep charcoal background, `#FFFFFF` text, high-contrast visual focus.
- **Count Tag:** Includes a micro-pill inside the button displaying item count (e.g., `Fridge 14`).

### 5. Fast-Logging Floating Action Button (FAB)
- **Styling:** High-visibility circle (56x56px) or pill with label ("+ Add Item"). Background `#059669`, text `#FFFFFF`, Level 3 emerald glow shadow.
- **Interaction:** Fixed at bottom-right on desktop, or centered floating dock on mobile. Quick-tap opens barcode scanner / voice logger; long-press opens manual entry sheet.

### 6. Kitchen High-Contrast Metric Counters
- **Usage:** Placed at the top of the dashboard to summarize current household status.
- **Style:** 3-column split card showing: `Fresh` (Count in Green), `Use Soon` (Count in Amber), and `Critical` (Count in Crimson Red).
- **Typography:** `counter-display` numbers paired with uppercase `label-xs` subheadings.
